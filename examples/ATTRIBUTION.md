# EuroSAT examples

These six JPEGs are unchanged copies of local EuroSAT RGB patches. Their original class labels, dataset-relative paths, and original split are recorded in `manifest.json`. They were selected for illustration from different classes without filtering for successful predictions. They are not an additional evaluation dataset; no sample-based accuracy is reported.

Source: [EuroSAT by Patrick Helber and colleagues](https://github.com/phelber/EuroSAT), derived from Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery. Please see the source's [dataset license and Sentinel data terms](https://github.com/phelber/EuroSAT#license).

Citation: Patrick Helber, Benjamin Bischke, Andreas Dengel, and Damian Borth. “EuroSAT: A Novel Dataset and Deep Learning Benchmark for Land Use and Land Cover Classification.” IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing, 2019.
